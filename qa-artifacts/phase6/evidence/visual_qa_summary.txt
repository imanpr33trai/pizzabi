---------------------------
- Raw enum / key leakage: grep over all captured *_ui.xml text/content-desc
  found zero occurrences of OUT_FOR_DELIVERY, COD_PENDING,
  AWAITING_CUSTOMER_PAYMENT, or any rzp_ value. Default customer text uses
  "Out for Delivery", "Pay on Delivery", "Waiting for payment confirmation...",
  etc. PASS.
- IST: order/order-history timestamps were rendered as "Today, 2:xx PM",
  "22 Nov, 3:10 PM", and "3:11 PM" while UTC was ~09:xx, consistent with
  Asia/Kolkata. Checkout exposed "Schedule Slot (IST)". Source formatter
  app/src/main/java/com/example/ui/components/OrderDisplayFormatters.kt uses
  TimeZone.getTimeZone("Asia/Kolkata"). PASS.
- Continuous stepper rails: pixel scan at x=84px on both stepper screenshots
  found an unbroken colored rail segment between each adjacent 24dp step
  circle (dark RGB 82,183,136; light RGB 45,106,79). PASS.
- Amber <=3m timer: normal 11:56 and expired 00:00 are visible. At the
  required 320dp/1.5x warning profile the amber banner and "2 minutes
  remaining" milestone are present, but the MM:SS node is absent. FAIL
  (DEFECT-1). At 320dp/1.0x diagnostic, "02:32" is visible.
- Payment CTA at 320dp/1.5x: bounds [40,960][600,1064]px = 280x52dp inside a
  640x1280 screen; CTA pixel fill dominates RGB (200,62,43) with white label
  pixels. Not clipped. PASS.
- Refund disclosure: CAPTURED Razorpay order PZ-P6-1002 produced the exact
  consumer-facing disclosure string. PASS.
- Celebration: PZ-P6-1006 was created through the app checkout flow; banner
  text appeared on Order Detail in both light 1.5x and dark 1.0x. PASS.

  prefix wraps).
